# 新卡面适配

先问用户希望植物居于哪个区域、希望信息优先还是图像优先。按目标构图生成一张独立的无字底图。

> Portrait 4:5 flat-lay artwork for premium botanical Christmas stationery. A clean warm-white fine cotton paper fills the frame. Photograph real evergreen, eucalyptus, holly, red winterberry, or pinecones under diffuse natural light. Compose [botanical grouping and location]. Reserve [explicit broad clean regions] for the title and activity details. Use understated greens, sparse reds, subtle grain and realistic contact shadows. Artwork only. No letters, numbers, printed frame, card edges, external backdrop, logos, snow or illustrations.

在配置中选对齐方式最接近的 `layout`，加入 `artwork`，用 `placements` 修正文字区域，再用 `focus` 对准一处辨识度高的枝叶或果实。在首帧检查四边满屏，在完整卡面检查细纸框和文字留白。用户确认新卡面后再渲染成片。参考[预览关键帧](../preview/frames.json)。
