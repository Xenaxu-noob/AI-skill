# Style Bible

## Visual direction

Flat-vector characters and typography over richer AI-generated background plates. The contrast is intentional: characters stay graphic and readable; environments carry texture and production value.

Keywords:

- flat fills, one darker tone per object, no outlines;
- muted dusty palette with one warm accent;
- soft film grain, low vignette;
- generous negative space for text;
- 2D camera drift, not 3D fly-throughs.

## Palette

Use a small, fixed set:

| Role | Example | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Background | dusty blue-grey, slate teal | rooms, sky, walls |
| Ink | near-black navy | body text, outlines-free shapes |
| Accent | salmon / coral | warnings, key objects |
| Warm | cream / beige | paper, cards, signs |
| Success | teal green | checks, “solved” |
| Highlight | yellow | numbers, punctuation, key labels |

Do not introduce a new saturated color for every shot. Consistency reads as quality.

## Typography

- Display Latin: Montserrat ExtraBold or similar geometric sans.
- CJK: Noto Sans SC Bold.
- Sizes: 24 / 32 / 44 / 64 / 88 px (choose 3 per film).
- Line height: 1.15–1.25.
- Letter spacing: 0 to +1 px; no condensed display except numbers.
- Text on screen: one line ≤ 14 CJK chars; two lines maximum.
- Captions: 1–2 lines, ≤ 42 Latin / 14 CJK chars per line.

## Characters

Keep a small cast:

1. **Robot** — the AI / product. Orange casing, dark face screen, cyan eyes.
2. **Viewer** — the human at home. Purple hoodie, dark pants, white sneakers, phone.
3. **Scientist / operator** — white coat, dark pants, glasses optional.
4. **Team** — 3–5 seated back-view figures, one accent color each.

Character rules:

- Hands visible; no armless silhouettes.
- Phone: audience sees the back, not the screen.
- Shoes: sole covers the pant-leg end; no dark cap below.
- Seated: body bottom on the seat; no floating or sinking.
- Back-view team: torso + arms is acceptable; no legs required if consistent.
- Faces: eyes with pupil/highlight, eyebrows, nose, mouth; avoid “two dots on a circle”.

## Props

Reusable set:

- phone, laptop, monitor, code window, browser card;
- book stack, open book, ladder, reading table;
- control console, big screen, status panels;
- easel, canvas, palette, typewriter;
- whiteboard, sticky notes, money notes, clock;
- desk, stool, sofa, lamp, plant.

Draw props flat. One darker tone for shadow; no gradients except soft light pools.

## Camera

- Default: 2–4% slow drift, one direction per shot.
- One special move per film: pullback, push-in, stack growth, or reveal.
- No handheld shake, no Dutch angle, no snap zooms.
- Keep text inside frame at all zoom levels; test first/mid/last frame.

## Transitions

- 80–90% hard cuts.
- 4–6 white splash wipes at act changes only.
- No cross-dissolves except a deliberate time jump.
- SFX: whoosh on wipes, pop on labels, chime on success.

## Text placement

- Axis-aligned to screen; never paste onto a perspective surface.
- If a plate surface is angled, replace it with a flat code panel or move text to a flat board.
- Card padding: 16–24 px; text never touches the border.
- Safe area: 80 px all sides for 16:9; 140 px top/bottom for 9:16.
- Center text horizontally and vertically in cards; check at first/mid/last frame.

## Grade and texture

- Slight saturation +2%, brightness +2%, contrast neutral.
- Vignette 0.03–0.06.
- Grain 0.10–0.14, animated, not static.
- Avoid crushed blacks; keep the night scenes readable.

## Sound

- One music bed, low-mid energy, no vocals.
- Duck music 12–15 dB under VO.
- SFX layers ≤ 3 at once.
- Final: −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1.5 dBTP (target −1.8).
