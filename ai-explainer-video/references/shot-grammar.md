# 10-Beat / 25-Shot Template

A 60–90 second film, average 2.2–3.2 s per shot. Adapt the content, keep the rhythm.

| # | Beat | Time (60 s) | Visual job | Text job |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| 5 | Reveal | 9.4–11.6 | Spotlight, nameplate | Name |

## Rules

- One idea per shot. If a shot needs two sentences, split it.
- First 3 seconds must contain a question, contradiction, or strong object.
- Do not put the product name after 15 seconds.
- Put the biggest visual event at 1/3 and 2/3 of the film.
- End on a readable frame, not a moving blur.
- Hold the final frame for at least 1.5 seconds.

## Variants

- 45 s: drop beats 3, 7, 18, 20; keep hook, reveal, mechanism, close.
- 90 s: add one proof beat and one caveat beat; keep cuts ≤ 3.5 s.
- 9:16: one subject per shot; move text to lower third; no wide group shots.
